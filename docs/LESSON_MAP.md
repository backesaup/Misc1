# Lesson map tracker

From Esa's Year 1–2 map (`briefs/lesson-map.pdf`): **familiarity and understanding over getting
answers right.** Open challenges with many right answers, estimates before counting, the world
shows how close he was, never marked wrong for a creative answer that works. Use the farm,
Minecraft blocks and Lego bricks. Go in any order.

Mechanics available now:
- **make** steps on `voxel:arrange` (modes `rows`, `pairs`, `split`, `tens`, `share`; items
  chicken, sheep, apple, brick, block): he picks a number, the world arranges it, leftovers glow,
  every way he finds is collected (`goal` = how many ways exist).
- **open** questions (`"open": true`): estimates; any guess is fine, then the reveal.
- `voxel:blocks64` (arrays, cubes, doubling), `voxel:disco` (light, sound), the farm game.
