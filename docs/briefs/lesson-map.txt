How to use this map
He was born in July, so he's in Year 1 now (2026–27) and Year 2 from September 2027. This
map covers Key Stage 1 maths and science, turned into lessons built around creativity
rather than getting answers right. He can already do much of the arithmetic; the aim is
understanding and play, with care and accuracy built in through the game.
Every lesson follows the Lesson Direction Brief and the Game Section brief. Each row
gives:
Big idea: the one concept the lesson is really about.
Creative challenge: an open task with many possible answers (low floor, high ceiling).
Where: Game (a farm mechanic), Lesson (a narrated breakdown), Short (a 30–60 s
maths short) or Real world (something to do off-screen).
Accuracy without drilling: in every challenge he estimates first, then the world shows
how close he was. The robot models checking its own work out loud. Mistakes are visible
and funny but cost nothing. Never mark him wrong for a creative answer that works, even
if it's not the one expected.
Year labels (Y1, Y2) show where each idea sits in the national curriculum. Go in any order
he's ready for; stretch items go beyond Year 2.

Maths: number, place value, adding and subtracting
Place value is the most important idea here. Being quick at sums doesn't guarantee he
knows why 34 means 3 tens and 4 ones, and everything later depends on it.

Science: Year 1 and Year 2
Key Stage 1 science covers plants, animals, materials, seasons and habitats. Beyond the
facts, the most important thing at this age is working scientifically: asking questions,
predicting, doing a simple test, observing and sorting. So every lesson here is an
experiment he runs, not facts to remember.
